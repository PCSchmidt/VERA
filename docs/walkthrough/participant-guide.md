# Trying VERA: a guide for you

Thank you for trying this. You are testing the software, not being tested. If something confuses you, that is the most useful thing you can
tell us, and it is not your mistake.

**What VERA is.** You give it a research topic. It proposes one question for you to confirm, searches and reads the literature, writes a
short review in which every claim is tied to a quote, audits the result, and shows you the audit. It runs on a model key (from a service
called OpenRouter) that you are given for this session. The cost is a few cents to a few tens of cents. You set a spending cap before each run.

**What you will do (about 45 minutes):**

1. Install and start it, using the README in the repository (https://github.com/PCSchmidt/VERA). If the README is not enough, say so and
   carry on as best you can; we will note where you got stuck.
2. Connect the key you were given, in the app's "Connect my key" box.
3. Pick a topic you know something about, from your own work or interests, and start a run. Choose a spending cap you are comfortable with.
4. When VERA proposes a question, decide whether it is the question you meant. Confirm it or edit it.
5. When the run finishes, read the review. Click a few dotted sentences and source numbers to see what they link to.
6. Answer four short questions at the end.

**Please think aloud** as you go: what you expect, what you are unsure of, what you would click next. The person observing will write
down what you say and where you pause. **They will not help you or explain anything while you work**, even if you ask. That is on purpose:
we need to see what the software shows a new person on its own. If you are truly stuck for more than a few minutes, say so and they will
note it and move you on.

**The four questions at the end:**

1. Whose money was used for your run? How do you know?
2. Did you trust the spend display? Why or why not?
3. Was the review worth reading? What would you want it to do better?
4. What, if anything, would stop you using VERA for your own work?

The key you were given is yours for this session only and will be switched off afterwards. VERA keeps it in memory and does not write it
to any file. The run's record (the topic you typed, the review, what it cost) is kept as evidence of the session, without your key; tell
the observer before you start if your topic is something you do not want kept.
